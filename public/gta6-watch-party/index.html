    Questions nobody asked

    FAQ

    Is this official?

    No. This is a fan-made parody page. Rockstar doesn't know we exist, which is sort of the theme of the evening.

    When exactly is the party?

    November 19, 2026 at midnight local time. That's when consoles unlock in each region, so the party rolls around the world one timezone at a time. You can arrive whenever you can stand it.

    Is GTA VI coming to PC?

    Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or a date. History says it usually happens eventually: GTA V reached PC about a year and a half after consoles, and Red Dead Redemption 2 took about a year. Until then, this lobby exists.

    Can my PS5 friend come?

    Yes. They are the content. Be supportive. Say "nice" about once every 33 milliseconds, which is one frame for them.

    What does Console Mode do?

    It caps this page at 30 FPS, makes your mouse cursor update at 30 FPS, and adds a little motion blur, so you can practise. We call it cinematic. You can also unlock it with a certain famous cheat code.

    What do you keep about me?

    Only what you type into the seat form: handle, rig, cope level and cope line. They're shown publicly on the wall. Spam limits use a scrambled code for your connection that changes every day and is deleted after two days. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking.

    Something on the wall is out of line. What now?

    The site owner can take any seat down. Get in touch through the main site.
